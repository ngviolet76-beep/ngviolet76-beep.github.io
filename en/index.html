Which method suits our building?

It depends on whether the building is new, whether the existing floor must be kept, and how much the floor height can be raised. The figures below are HiHousing's listed values.

Dry (carbon fiber)RemodelingRises 1.5–2 cm
Wet (carbon fiber)New constructionRises approx. 41.3–46.3 mm
Cutting (carbon fiber)Areas with heavy furniture or high trafficNo change in height

Dry installation follows these steps

FAQ

Common questions from facility managers

How do I choose a good carbon floor heating installer?

Judge by documents and records rather than by name. Check five things: who does the electrical work, whether there are product safety documents, whether a method suited to the building is proposed, whether there are records for the same type of building, and whether the warranty scope is given in writing.

How can I verify that a contractor's certifications are genuine?

Get the issuing body, number, and validity period of the certificate, and verify with the issuer. HiHousing holds Certified Social Enterprise No. 2021-222 (Minister of Employment and Labor) and is registered for competitive bidding with the Public Procurement Service.

What is the process for introducing it at a school or public institution?

Purchasing methods depend on each institution's rules, so please check with the responsible department first. HiHousing is registered for competitive bidding with the Public Procurement Service. Tell us the building type and work conditions, and we can look at the right method together.

What areas do you serve?

We are headquartered in Jeonju, Jeonbuk, and install nationwide in South Korea.
