FACILITIES

Floor Heating Installation for Schools, Public Facilities, Nursing Homes, and Kindergartens Nationwide in South Korea

Methods and checklists for facility managers responsible for floors used by students, seniors, and children.

Ask about facility floor heating

Who this is for

  • Those replacing heating in school classrooms, libraries, night-duty rooms, or after-school care rooms
  • Those managing floors in care facilities such as nursing homes and kindergartens
  • Those preparing heating work at public institutions and facilities

Common concerns

It is hard to confirm that a contractor's certifications are genuine, and safety is a concern for floors used by students, seniors, and children. The scale and inconvenience of the work, and who is responsible for breakdowns, should be settled in advance.

Five things to check before contracting

  1. Electrical work
    Check who does the electrical work and whether they are a registered contractor.
  2. Product safety documents
    Obtain product test and certification documents and verify the numbers.
  3. Method proposal
    See whether they propose a method suited to the building and explain why.
  4. Installation records
    Ask whether they have records of installing in the same type of facility.
  5. Written warranty
    Put the warranty scope and responsible party in the contract.

What we provide and scope

We choose among dry, wet, cutting, and film installation based on new build or remodeling and how the space is used. Floor height changes and installation steps by method can be compared on the Services page.

Purchasing methods depend on each institution's rules, so please check with the responsible department first.

What to prepare before asking

  • Facility type and the space to be installed (classrooms, living rooms, etc.)
  • New construction or remodeling
  • Whether the facility must stay in use during the work

Precautions

Whatever the method, building and electrical conditions must be examined first. Safety and heating performance depend on the product and installation conditions, so we recommend confirming with documents and installation records.

FAQ

Is it safe to install carbon floor heating in a nursing home?

This must be judged from the building, electrical equipment, and product documents, and cannot be stated in a single word. Nursing homes are among the buildings HiHousing serves. Please also check the work schedule while residents live there, product documents, and breaker and grounding details.

Is carbon film suitable for daycare or kindergarten floor heating?

Kindergartens are among the buildings HiHousing serves. The listed operating temperature of PTC graphene carbon film is 50–60℃. Decide whether it suits your facility by reviewing the space, floor finish, and product documents together.
