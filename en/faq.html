FAQ

Carbon floor heating: common questions from facility managers

From how to choose a contractor to what to check by facility, here is what people ask before contracting.

Ask by phone now

Choosing a contractor

How do I choose a good carbon floor heating installer?

Judge by documents and records rather than by name. Check five things: who does the electrical work, whether there are product safety documents, whether a method suited to the building is proposed, whether there are records for the same type of building, and whether the warranty scope is given in writing.

How can I verify that a contractor's certifications are genuine?

Get the issuing body, number, and validity period of the certificate, and verify with the issuer. HiHousing holds Certified Social Enterprise No. 2021-222 (Minister of Employment and Labor) and is registered for competitive bidding with the Public Procurement Service.

Installation by facility

Is it safe to install carbon floor heating in a nursing home?

This must be judged from the building, electrical equipment, and product documents, and cannot be stated in a single word. Nursing homes are among the buildings HiHousing serves. Please also check the work schedule while residents live there, product documents, and breaker and grounding details.

Is carbon film suitable for daycare or kindergarten floor heating?

Kindergartens are among the buildings HiHousing serves. The listed operating temperature of PTC graphene carbon film is 50–60℃. Decide whether it suits your facility by reviewing the space, floor finish, and product documents together.

What is the process for introducing carbon floor heating at a school or public institution?

Purchasing methods depend on each institution's rules, so please check with the responsible department first. HiHousing is registered for competitive bidding with the Public Procurement Service.

Methods and scope

Which method should I choose?

By HiHousing's listing, dry suits remodeling, wet suits new construction, and cutting suits areas with heavy furniture or high traffic. PTC film (linear and planar) is used for remodeling.

How much does the floor rise after installation?

Dry raises it 1.5–2 cm, wet approx. 41.3–46.3 mm, and cutting not at all. PTC film raises it approx. 0.68–0.69 cm with wood or vinyl tile and approx. 0.48–0.49 cm with laminate.

What areas do you serve?

We are headquartered in Jeonju, Jeonbuk, and install nationwide in South Korea.
