ABOUT

A Jeonju company that takes care of what lies beneath your floor

HiHousing Co., Ltd. is a floor heating specialist and certified social enterprise headquartered in Jeonju, Jeonbuk, installing carbon floor heating at sites across South Korea.

Installation & Quote +82-10-4524-2287

WHAT WE DO

What does the company do?

HiHousing is an eco-friendly social venture that improves living conditions with carbon heating elements and makes carbon-based products. We install floor heating, in which carbon materials produce heat when powered by electricity, using the method that suits each building.

We install "The Go-On" carbon fiber floor heating and PTC graphene carbon film. Carbon fiber is installed by dry, wet, or cutting methods; carbon film in linear or planar form.

Those who need this work most are facility managers at schools, nursing homes, and daycare centers. People responsible for floors used by students, seniors, and children must check a contractor's certifications and post-installation responsibility.

Where is the head office?

We are at Room 205, Korea Carbon Industry Promotion Agency, 106 Wonmanseong-ro (Palbok-dong 2-ga), Deokjin-gu, Jeonju-si, Jeollabuk-do, South Korea. We install nationwide in South Korea.

How are we different from similar companies?

We work with three carbon fiber methods and two PTC film types, so you can choose what suits your building. Part of our installation revenue funds donated heating for energy-vulnerable households.
