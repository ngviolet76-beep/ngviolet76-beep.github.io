PTC FILM

PTC Graphene Carbon Film Floor Heating Installation Nationwide in South Korea (Linear, Planar)

Thin carbon film is laid beneath the floor finish, in linear or planar form, to suit remodeled spaces.

Ask about carbon film floor heating

Product overview

A heating element made of PET film coated with graphene carbon powder. Available as linear film and planar film, for professional installation or self-installation.

  • Heat output self-regulates as resistance rises with temperature
  • Under 0.5 mm thick, suited to remodeled spaces
  • Graphene is a material with high electrical conductivity and strength (company description)

Who this is for

  • Those adding floor heating during remodeling
  • Those who want to keep the floor height increase under 1 cm
  • Those choosing an installation type to match wood, vinyl, tile, or laminate finishes

Common concerns

It is difficult to greatly change the floor height of an existing space, and hard to tell whether a film product suits your finish.

Materials used

  • Insulation

    1 m × 50 m, 4.5 mm thick. Blocks heat loss to the floor below and moisture.

  • Linear film heating element

    Heating element coated with PTC carbon powder. 0.338 mm thick, 50–60℃ operating temperature.

  • Planar film heating element

    Heating element coated with PTC carbon powder. 0.4 mm thick, 50–60℃ operating temperature.

  • Fiber protective board

    1 m × 1.2 m, 2 mm thick. Reduces rapid temperature changes.

  • Thermostat

    Front-mounted 4 kW, 10-level temperature control, flame-retardant PC/ABS housing.

What to prepare before asking

  • Building type and how the space is used
  • Floor finish to be used
  • How much the floor height can be raised

Precautions

Film installation raises the floor slightly, depending on the finish. Heating performance and running costs may vary by building conditions.

FAQ

What is the difference between linear and planar?

Their listed thicknesses differ: 0.338 mm for linear and 0.4 mm for planar. Power consumption by width also differs, as shown in the table above.

What spaces is it used in?

HiHousing lists the linear and planar types of PTC film as suited to remodeling.
