PRINCIPLES

Three principles of installation

  • We look at the building first

    We choose the method based on new build or remodeling, traffic, and how much the floor height can be raised.

  • We verify with a heating test

    Every dry, wet, cutting, and film sequence includes a heating test. With the wet method, we test before pouring cement.

  • We share part of our revenue

    Part of our installation revenue funds donated heating for energy-vulnerable households.
