CARBON FIBER

Carbon Fiber Floor Heating Installation Nationwide in South Korea (Dry, Wet, Cutting)

Heating in which carbon fiber produces heat when powered, installed by whichever of three methods suits your building.

Ask about carbon fiber floor heating

Product overview

The Go-On reinvents Korea's traditional ondol as a modern floor heating system. Carbon fiber produces heat when powered, and it can be installed by dry, wet, or cutting methods.

  • Uses carbon fiber with 92% or more carbon content
  • Parallel wiring heats the entire floor evenly in about 10 minutes (company figure)
  • At least 90.4% of radiant heat is far-infrared (company figure)

Who this is for

  • Those remodeling an existing building and adding new heating
  • Those choosing floor heating for a new building
  • Those managing spaces where floor height cannot change, or with heavy furniture and high traffic

Common concerns

The scale and inconvenience of floor work are a burden, and it is hard to tell which method suits your building. You also need to know in advance how much the floor height will change.

Cutting installation

Materials used

  • Insulation

    1 m × 50 m, 4.5 mm or 8 mm thick. Blocks heat loss to the floor below and moisture.

  • Carbon fiber heating element

    24K (24,000 strands) carbon fiber, heat-resistant silicone coating. Dry 28 W/m, wet and cutting 32 W/m.

  • Aluminum heat-spreading sheet

    0.6 m × 50 m, 0.3 mm thick. High-purity aluminum spreads heat evenly.

  • Fiber protective board

    1 m × 1.2 m, 2 mm thick. Reduces rapid temperature changes.

  • Fiberglass mesh

    1 m × 50 m, 1 mm thick. Prevents cement cracks.

  • Galvanized steel sheet

    0.6 m × 1.2 m, 0.8 mm thick. Supports floor finishing and protects the heating element.

  • Thermostat

    Front-mounted 4 kW, 10-level temperature control, flame-retardant PC/ABS housing.

What to prepare before asking

  • Building type and whether it is new construction or remodeling
  • How the space is used, and whether it has heavy furniture or high traffic
  • Whether the floor height can be raised

Precautions

Dry and wet methods raise the floor. The cutting method does not change the height but requires cutting grooves in the floor. Heating performance and running costs may vary by building conditions.

FAQ

Which method suits remodeling?

HiHousing lists the dry method for remodeling. If the floor height cannot change or traffic is heavy, consider cutting as well.

Does the whole floor have to be removed?

It depends on the method. The dry method builds layers up from the insulation; the cutting method places heating elements in grooves cut into the floor.
